# Steam activity and popularity snapshot

This is a dated, **independent of reviews** companion to the `Popularity` column in [Possible Games and Early Access](../games/README.md). The source is Steam Charts’ per-app **“Last 30 Days → Avg. Players”** (rolling average of simultaneous Steam players), primarily retrieved October 26, 2026 Pacific time; individual publicly indexed pages have different crawl timestamps and may lag. This is not sales, unique players, ownership, ratings, co-op-specific activity, or users playing from other launchers. SteamDB player histories may be used as a cross-check, but Steam Charts’ public 30-day averages are the primary source for this snapshot.

## Star thresholds

| Display | 30-day average concurrent Steam players | Interpretation |
| :---: | ---: | :--- |
| ☆☆☆ | 0 to under 100 | Very small current Steam audience |
| ★☆☆ | 100 to under 1,000 | Smaller active Steam audience |
| ★★☆ | 1,000 to under 10,000 | Established active Steam audience |
| ★★★ | 10,000 or more | Large active Steam audience |
| `n/a` | Unavailable or too stale to use | No verified score; **not** the same as zero stars |

- Assign stars by comparing the **underlying nonrounded average**, not the number of Steam reviews, review percent, daily peak, current player count or all-time peak. Maintain existing catalog order by Percent then exact review Count; the popularity column does not control sorting.
- Check data for each listed Steam app ID individually. A shared launcher/hub may count multiple games; never assign its total to a particular title. Mark inaccessible, genuinely unavailable or more-than-30-days-stale sources `n/a` until a reliable 30-day average is verified. On refresh, update checked/retrieval dates and preserve caveats for sources that have not been refreshed.
- When refreshing games, recheck the 30-day average for **all** active Possible Games and Early Access entries, and update this file and the stars together. For approved additions, add an activity row if verified, otherwise `n/a` with a reason. Retain review snapshot dates and figures separately.
